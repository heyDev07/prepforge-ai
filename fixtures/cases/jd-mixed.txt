Data Engineer — Harbor Logistics, Rotterdam

Harbor Logistics builds software that freight forwarders use to quote, book and track ocean and air shipments.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain batch and streaming data pipelines for shipment tracking events
- Model data in our warehouse to support pricing and on-time delivery reporting
- Work with operations teams to define data quality checks

Requirements
- 3+ years of experience as a data engineer
- Strong SQL and Python
- Experience with Apache Airflow or a similar orchestration tool
- Experience with a cloud data warehouse such as BigQuery or Snowflake
- Understanding of freight forwarding or supply chain operations
- Knowledge of customs and trade documentation (e.g. bills of lading)
- Ability to explain data findings to non-technical operations teams

It is a plus if you have experience with Kafka.
