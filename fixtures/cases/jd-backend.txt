Senior Backend Engineer, Fleet Platform — Acme Robotics
Berlin, Germany or remote within the EU

About the role
Acme Robotics builds autonomous mobile robots for warehouses. The Fleet Platform team owns the services that assign tasks to thousands of robots in real time.

What you will do
- Design, build and operate Node.js services that coordinate robot fleets
- Own the reliability of event-driven systems built on Kafka
- Improve latency and throughput of our task-assignment APIs
- Review code and mentor other engineers on the team

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional backend development experience
- Strong TypeScript and Node.js skills
- Experience designing relational schemas and tuning queries in PostgreSQL
- Experience with event-driven architectures and message brokers such as Kafka
- Experience running services in production on Kubernetes
- Clear written communication, including design documents
- A track record of mentoring engineers

Nice to have
- Experience with Go
- Background in robotics, warehouse automation or logistics

We offer 30 days of paid leave, a learning budget and hardware of your choice.
