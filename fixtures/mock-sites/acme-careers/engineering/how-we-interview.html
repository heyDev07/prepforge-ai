How we interview engineers

  1. A 30-minute call with a recruiter about your background and what you are looking for.
  2. A 90-minute technical session: either a take-home exercise (about 3 hours) or live pairing on a small service.
  3. A system design conversation about a real problem, such as assigning tasks to a fleet of robots.
  4. A values interview with an engineering manager focused on ownership and collaboration.

The process usually takes two to three weeks and we give written feedback after the technical stage.
