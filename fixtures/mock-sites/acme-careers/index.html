Robots that keep mid-size warehouses moving

Acme Robotics builds autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and the fleet-management software that coordinates them. Our robots move totes between storage and packing stations so that pickers walk less and ship more.

More than 140 warehouses across Europe and North America run on Acme Fleet, our cloud platform for routing, task assignment and real-time monitoring of robot fleets.
