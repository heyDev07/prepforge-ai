About Acme Robotics

Founded in 2017 in Berlin, Acme Robotics now has offices in Berlin and Austin and a team of about 180 people.

Our mission is to make warehouse work safer and more efficient by automating the walking, not the people.

What we value

  • Ownership: engineers run what they build.
  • Safety first: robots share floors with people.
  • Customer obsession: we ship to real warehouses every two weeks.
